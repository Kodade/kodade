You may:

- truthfully state that your software uses, integrates with, or is based on
  Ködade;
- link to the official project or reproduce an unmodified screenshot for
  commentary, documentation, or news; and
- use the name to describe an unmodified official Ködade release.

## Uses that require permission

Do not use the Ködade name or logos in a way that suggests an unofficial fork,
product, service, domain, account, or organization is produced, sponsored, or
endorsed by the Ködade project.

Forks and modified distributions must use a distinct product name and visual
identity. They may describe themselves as “based on Ködade” and must clearly
identify their modifications.

fair use or nominative use.
